    ~Behind the camera~

    I am Balázs Kepenyes, a freelance photographer. For me, photography is more than just pressing a button; it's about capturing unrepeatable moments and genuine emotions. Whether it's a dynamic sports event, an unforgettable party, or the most important day of your life your wedding my goal is always to tell a real story through my images.

    In my work, I strive for a natural, clean, and premium visual style. I know that standing in front of a camera can often be intimidating, which is why creating a relaxed, stress-free atmosphere during my shoots is paramount. I believe the most beautiful shots are born when you let go and can truly be yourself.

    The booking timeframe depends on the type of photoshoot. For weddings and larger events, it's recommended to reach out 6–12 months before the big day, as spring and summer weekends fill up quickly. For smaller sessions (such as portrait, sports, or couples photography), reaching out 2–4 weeks in advance is usually sufficient. In any case, I recommend contacting me as soon as you have an idea in mind so we can secure the perfect date!

    Yes, your date becomes officially finalized and guaranteed in my calendar upon the payment of a deposit (and the acceptance of a contract for larger events). The deposit is typically 20–30% of the total amount, which will naturally be deducted from the final cost of the photoshoot. Until this is completed, I will hold the date tentatively, but it remains open to other inquiries.

    I understand that unexpected things can happen (such as illness or bad weather for outdoor shoots). I am flexible in these situations: you have the option to reschedule your session once, free of charge, provided you notify me at least 48 hours in advance. However, in the case of a permanent cancellation or last-minute rescheduling, the deposit is unfortunately non-refundable, as I can no longer offer that time slot to someone else.

    The remaining balance can be settled in cash on the day of the photoshoot or via bank transfer (no later than the delivery of the final edited photos). I issue an invoice for all my services, making corporate bank transfers seamless for businesses and companies.

If you're unsure, I’m more than happy to help coordinate your outfits before the shoot!
